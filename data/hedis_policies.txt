HEDIS QUALITY MEASURES - POLICIES AND GUIDELINES

=== MEASURE DEFINITIONS ===

BCS (Breast Cancer Screening)
- Target Population: Women ages 50-74
- Requirement: Mammogram within 27 months
- Compliance Rate Goal: ≥ 75%
- Gap Closure Window: Must complete by 12/31 for current year credit
- Qualifying CPT Codes: 77067, 77063 (digital mammography)
- Exclusions:
  * Bilateral mastectomy (any time in history)
  * Unilateral mastectomy with history of bilateral breast cancer
  * Advanced illness with limited life expectancy
- Member outreach: Phone call + mailed reminder with scheduling assistance
- Average gap closure rate: 65% with proactive outreach
- Revenue impact per gap closed: ~$100

CDC (Comprehensive Diabetes Care)
- Target Population: Members ages 18-75 with Type 1 or Type 2 diabetes
- Requirements (at least one of each per year):
  * HbA1c test (CPT: 83036, 83037) - at least one per year
  * Eye exam (retinal) - CPT: 67028, 67030 - within 24 months
  * Medical attention for nephropathy - kidney screening
  * Blood pressure control (< 140/90 mmHg)
- Compliance Rate Goal: ≥ 80% for HbA1c testing
- High-weight measure: 3x multiplier in star rating
- Exclusions: 
  * Advanced illness with limited life expectancy
  * Palliative care
  * Diabetes diagnosis in last 60 days of measurement year
- Member outreach: Multi-component (PCP coordination + member education)
- Average gap closure rate: 72% with coordinated care
- Revenue impact per gap closed: ~$150 (high-weight measure)

CBP (Controlling High Blood Pressure)
- Target Population: Adults ages 18-85 with hypertension diagnosis
- Requirement: Most recent BP reading < 140/90 mmHg
- Compliance Rate Goal: ≥ 70%
- High-weight measure: 3x multiplier in star rating
- Gap Closure Requirements:
  * Must have in-person visit with BP measurement
  * BP reading must be documented in medical record
  * Most recent reading within measurement year counts
- Exclusions:
  * Advanced illness with limited life expectancy
  * End-stage kidney disease with dialysis
  * Kidney transplant
- Member outreach: PCP visit scheduling + home BP monitoring education
- Average gap closure rate: 68%
- Revenue impact per gap closed: ~$150 (high-weight measure)

COL (Colorectal Cancer Screening)
- Target Population: Adults ages 50-75 (expanded to 45-75 for 2024)
- Requirements (any one of the following):
  * Fecal occult blood test (FOBT) - annual
  * FIT-DNA test - every 3 years
  * Colonoscopy - every 10 years
  * Flexible sigmoidoscopy - every 5 years
  * CT colonography - every 5 years
- Compliance Rate Goal: ≥ 70%
- High-weight measure: 3x multiplier in star rating
- Exclusions:
  * Total colectomy (any time in history)
  * Colorectal cancer diagnosis
  * Advanced illness with limited life expectancy
- Member outreach: FIT kit mailing program (most cost-effective)
- Average gap closure rate: 55% (lower due to member reluctance)
- Revenue impact per gap closed: ~$150 (high-weight measure)

OMW (Osteoporosis Management in Women)
- Target Population: Women ages 67-85 who had a fracture
- Requirement: DXA scan or osteoporosis medication within 6 months of fracture
- Compliance Rate Goal: ≥ 65%
- Medium-weight measure: 2x multiplier in star rating
- Gap Closure Window: 6 months from fracture date
- Qualifying Services:
  * DXA scan (CPT: 77080, 77081)
  * Osteoporosis medications (bisphosphonates, etc.)
- Exclusions: Advanced illness with limited life expectancy
- Member outreach: PCP coordination within 2 weeks of fracture
- Average gap closure rate: 70%
- Revenue impact per gap closed: ~$100

=== GAP CLOSURE PROCEDURES ===

Step 1: Identify Open Gaps
- Run members_with_gap('MEASURE_CODE') to get list
- Prioritize by:
  1. High-weight measures (CDC, CBP, COL) - 3x impact
  2. Days until due (< 60 days = high priority)
  3. Member engagement history (responsive members first)
  4. Risk score (high-risk members = higher impact)

Step 2: Member Outreach
- Phone call (3 attempts over 2 weeks):
  * Explain the measure and importance
  * Offer scheduling assistance
  * Address barriers (transportation, cost, understanding)
- If no phone contact:
  * Mailed letter with measure explanation
  * Educational materials in preferred language
  * Phone number for scheduling assistance
- Documentation required:
  * Date and time of each outreach attempt
  * Member response or reason for no contact
  * Scheduled appointment date (if applicable)

Step 3: Service Delivery Coordination
- PCP coordination:
  * Fax gap list to PCP office
  * Request appointment scheduling
  * Confirm member will be seen
- For non-visit services (BCS, COL):
  * Direct member to radiology/lab
  * Confirm appointment scheduled
  * Send reminder 2 days before appointment
- Member barriers:
  * Transportation: Arrange medical transportation
  * Cost: Verify zero copay for preventive services
  * Language: Provide interpreter services
  * Health literacy: Simplified educational materials

Step 4: Service Completion
- Complete required screening/test
- Document in medical record with:
  * Correct CPT code
  * Date of service within measurement year
  * Results (if applicable)
- Ensure claims submission within 30 days
- Verify claim includes:
  * Valid CPT code for measure
  * Diagnosis code supporting measure eligibility
  * In-network provider (or authorized out-of-network)

Step 5: Verification and Follow-up
- Confirm claim received and processed (7-14 day lag)
- Update gap status in tracking system
- Calculate updated compliance rate
- If gap persists:
  * Verify CPT code correctness
  * Check for claim processing errors
  * Resubmit if necessary
- Member follow-up:
  * Thank member for completing service
  * Provide results explanation (if appropriate)
  * Schedule follow-up if needed

=== NCQA COMPLIANCE REQUIREMENTS ===

Documentation Standards:
- All services must have valid CPT/HCPCS codes per HEDIS specifications
- Date of service must be within measurement year (January 1 - December 31)
- Provider must be in network (or authorized out-of-network)
- Medical records must be available for audit (retain 10 years)
- Electronic records preferred, paper acceptable with proper indexing

Reporting Timeline:
- Measurement Year: January 1 - December 31
- Data collection cutoff: February 28 (following year)
- Supplemental data deadline: March 31
- Final submission to NCQA: June 30
- NCQA audit period: August - October
- Star ratings published: October 15

Audit Preparation:
- Random sample selection by NCQA (typically 30 records per measure)
- Must produce medical records within 30 days of request
- Records must clearly document:
  * Date of service
  * Type of service (CPT code evident)
  * Provider credentials
  * Member identification
- Common audit failures:
  * Missing documentation
  * Service date outside measurement year
  * Wrong CPT code (non-qualifying service)
  * Provider not credentialed

Quality Thresholds (5-Star Rating):
- 5 Stars: ≥ 90% compliance
- 4 Stars: 80-89% compliance
- 3 Stars: 70-79% compliance
- 2 Stars: 60-69% compliance
- 1 Star: < 60% compliance

Star Rating Calculation:
- Weighted average of all HEDIS measures
- High-weight measures (CDC, CBP, COL): 3x multiplier
- Medium-weight measures (BCS, OMW): 2x multiplier
- Standard measures: 1x multiplier
- Overall score = weighted sum / total weight
- Rounded to nearest 0.5 stars

=== MEASURE WEIGHTS (STAR RATING) ===

High Impact (3x weight):
- CDC (Comprehensive Diabetes Care)
  * Rationale: High-cost condition, significant health impact
  * Population size: Typically 15-20% of membership
  * Improvement potential: High (many opportunities)

- CBP (Controlling Blood Pressure)
  * Rationale: Leading cause of cardiovascular events
  * Population size: Typically 25-35% of membership
  * Improvement potential: Medium (requires clinical intervention)

- COL (Colorectal Cancer Screening)
  * Rationale: Preventable cancer, high mortality if unscreened
  * Population size: Typically 30-40% of membership
  * Improvement potential: Medium (member engagement challenge)

Medium Impact (2x weight):
- BCS (Breast Cancer Screening)
  * Rationale: Early detection reduces mortality
  * Population size: Typically 15-20% of female membership
  * Improvement potential: High (established screening program)

- OMW (Osteoporosis Management in Women)
  * Rationale: Fracture prevention, quality of life
  * Population size: Small (2-5% of eligible women)
  * Improvement potential: High (timely intervention critical)

Standard Impact (1x weight):
- All other HEDIS measures
- Lower population prevalence or newer measures

=== REVENUE IMPACT ===

Star Rating Impact on Revenue:
- 5 Stars: 5% bonus payment on total Medicare Advantage premium
- 4 Stars: 3% bonus payment
- 3 Stars: Base payment (no bonus)
- 2 Stars: -2% penalty
- 1 Star: -5% penalty

Per-Member-Per-Month (PMPM) Impact:
- Each 0.1 star improvement ≈ $1.50 PMPM across all members
- For 100,000 members:
  * 0.1 star = $180K monthly = $2.16M annually
  * 0.5 star = $900K monthly = $10.8M annually
  * 1.0 star = $1.8M monthly = $21.6M annually

Gap Closure Value by Measure Weight:
High-weight measure gap closed:
  - Impact on star rating: 0.003 per member (approximate)
  - Revenue impact: ~$150 per gap closed
  - Example: 1000 CDC gaps closed = $150K annual revenue

Medium-weight measure gap closed:
  - Impact on star rating: 0.002 per member
  - Revenue impact: ~$100 per gap closed
  - Example: 1000 BCS gaps closed = $100K annual revenue

Standard measure gap closed:
  - Impact on star rating: 0.001 per member
  - Revenue impact: ~$50 per gap closed

Investment ROI:
- Outreach cost per gap closed: ~$25-50
- Service cost covered by capitation (already budgeted)
- Net ROI: 2:1 to 6:1 depending on measure weight
- Breakeven: Close 250-500 gaps to pay for gap closure program

Strategic Priorities (ROI-based):
1. High-weight measures with high gap counts (CDC, CBP, COL)
2. Members with multiple gaps (highest efficiency)
3. Gaps close to measurement year end (<90 days)
4. Historically responsive members (higher success rate)
5. Medium-weight measures with quick wins (BCS)

=== PERFORMANCE BENCHMARKING ===

National Benchmarks (Medicare Advantage, 2024):
- BCS: 75-80% (top performers: 85%+)
- CDC HbA1c: 85-90% (top performers: 92%+)
- CBP: 65-70% (top performers: 75%+)
- COL: 70-75% (top performers: 80%+)
- OMW: 60-65% (top performers: 70%+)

Regional Variation:
- Urban vs Rural: 5-10% difference (urban higher)
- Socioeconomic factors: Significant impact
- Provider density: Higher density = better performance
- Health literacy: Education level correlation

Humana Quality Goals (2024):
- Overall star rating: 4.0 or higher
- High-weight measures: All above 75%
- Medium-weight measures: All above 70%
- Zero measures below 60% (avoid 2-star territory)

=== COMMON SCENARIOS AND RESOLUTIONS ===

Scenario 1: Claim processed but gap still shows open
Resolution:
- Check claim processing date (up to 14-day lag in gap closure update)
- Verify CPT code matches HEDIS specifications
- Confirm date of service is within measurement year
- Check for duplicate or corrected claims
- Contact claims department if over 30 days old

Scenario 2: Member reports service completed but no claim found
Resolution:
- Verify service was with in-network provider
- Check if provider has submitted claim (contact provider)
- If provider hasn't submitted, request expedited submission
- If claim denied, review denial reason and appeal if appropriate
- Consider supplemental data submission if after February cutoff

Scenario 3: Member refuses screening
Resolution:
- Document refusal in medical record with date and reason
- Provide education on importance and risks of non-screening
- Offer alternatives if available (e.g., FIT kit instead of colonoscopy)
- Attempt outreach again in 3-6 months
- Note: Refusal does NOT close gap for HEDIS purposes

Scenario 4: Member has exclusion but gap still shows
Resolution:
- Verify exclusion diagnosis is coded in claims
- Check if exclusion is permanent (e.g., mastectomy) or temporary
- Submit supplemental data with exclusion documentation
- Update member record to prevent future gap alerts

Scenario 5: Service completed outside measurement year
Resolution:
- If before January 1: May count for prior year if within specification window
- If after December 31: Counts for next measurement year only
- Cannot be counted retroactively in current year
- Emphasize timing importance to member and provider
