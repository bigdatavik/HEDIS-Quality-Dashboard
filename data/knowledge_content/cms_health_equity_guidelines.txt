CMS HEALTH EQUITY GUIDELINES
Healthcare Effectiveness and Health Equity Framework

Last Updated: October 2025
Source: Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services

==================================================
OVERVIEW
==================================================

The CMS Framework for Health Equity 2022-2032 establishes a roadmap for advancing health equity across Medicare, Medicaid, and CHIP programs.

Key Goals:
- Expand collection, reporting, and analysis of standardized data
- Assess causes of disparities within CMS programs  
- Build capacity of healthcare organizations to reduce disparities
- Develop and support a diverse, culturally competent workforce
- Improve language access and health literacy

==================================================
HEALTH EQUITY DEFINED
==================================================

Health Equity: The state in which everyone has a fair and just opportunity to attain their highest level of health.

Health Disparities: Preventable differences in the burden of disease, injury, violence, or opportunities to achieve optimal health experienced by socially disadvantaged populations.

Social Determinants of Health (SDOH): The conditions in which people are born, grow, work, live, and age, and the wider set of forces and systems shaping daily life.

==================================================
CMS HEALTH EQUITY PRIORITY AREAS
==================================================

Priority 1: Expand Data Collection and Reporting
- Collect standardized demographic data (race, ethnicity, language, disability, sexual orientation, gender identity)
- Stratify quality measures by demographic groups
- Publicly report disparities data

Priority 2: Assess Payment and Benefit Design
- Evaluate how payment models affect equity
- Adjust risk adjustment for social factors
- Incentivize disparity reduction

Priority 3: Build Capacity
- Provide technical assistance to providers
- Fund disparity reduction grants
- Develop health equity learning networks

Priority 4: Advance Language Access and Health Literacy
- Require provision of interpreter services
- Provide culturally appropriate education materials
- Assess health literacy levels

Priority 5: Increase Workforce Diversity
- Support training for underrepresented minorities
- Incentivize culturally competent care
- Address workforce shortages in underserved areas

==================================================
MEDICARE ADVANTAGE HEALTH EQUITY REQUIREMENTS
==================================================

Effective 2024:
- MA plans must collect and report race, ethnicity, and language data
- Star Ratings include health equity index
- Plans must conduct health equity assessments
- Disparity reduction plans required for low-performing plans

Health Equity Measures in Star Ratings (2024+):
1. Screening for Social Needs
2. Screen Positive Rate and Interventions
3. Race/Ethnicity Stratification for Quality Measures
4. Reduction in Disparity Gaps Over Time

==================================================
SCREENING FOR SOCIAL NEEDS
==================================================

Recommended Tools:
- PRAPARE (Protocol for Responding to and Assessing Patients' Assets, Risks, and Experiences)
- AHC-HRSN (Accountable Health Communities Health-Related Social Needs)
- CMS Screening Tool

Core Domains to Screen:
1. Food Insecurity
2. Housing Instability
3. Transportation Barriers
4. Utility Assistance Needs
5. Interpersonal Safety

Implementation:
- Screen all members annually
- Document screening results
- Develop intervention plans for screen-positive members
- Track resolution of social needs

==================================================
INTERVENTION STRATEGIES
==================================================

For Food Insecurity:
- Connect to SNAP (Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program)
- Meals on Wheels referral
- Food pantry partnerships
- Medically tailored meals post-discharge

For Housing Instability:
- Housing assistance program referrals
- Homeless services coordination
- Temporary housing support

For Transportation:
- Non-emergency medical transportation (NEMT)
- Ride-share partnerships (Uber Health, Lyft)
- Public transportation vouchers
- Volunteer driver programs

For Health Literacy:
- Translated materials in threshold languages
- Pictorial/visual aids
- Teach-back methods
- Health coaching

==================================================
DISPARITY MEASUREMENT
==================================================

Calculate Disparity Ratios:
Disparity Ratio = (Rate in Disadvantaged Group) / (Rate in Reference Group)

Example:
- White beneficiaries: 88% diabetes control
- Black beneficiaries: 78% diabetes control
- Disparity Ratio: 78% / 88% = 0.89

Target: Disparity ratio ≥ 0.90 (less than 10% gap)

Report Disparities Stratified By:
- Race (White, Black, Hispanic, Asian, Native American, Other)
- Ethnicity (Hispanic/Latino, Non-Hispanic)
- Language (English, Spanish, Other)
- Dual Eligible Status (Medicare + Medicaid)
- Disability Status
- Geography (Rural vs Urban)

==================================================
HEALTH EQUITY BONUS PAYMENTS (2025+)
==================================================

CMS will provide bonus payments for:
1. Plans serving high proportions of beneficiaries with social risk factors
2. Plans demonstrating measurable disparity reduction
3. Plans implementing evidence-based equity interventions

Potential Bonus: +0.5 star rating boost or additional 2% payment

Eligibility Criteria:
- Collect complete demographic data (>95% of members)
- Screen all members for social needs
- Document interventions for screen-positive members
- Show year-over-year disparity reduction
- Submit health equity improvement plan

==================================================
NCQA HEALTH EQUITY ACCREDITATION (HEA)
==================================================

Voluntary Accreditation Program
Demonstrates commitment to reducing disparities

Requirements:
1. Leadership Commitment
   - Board-level oversight
   - Dedicated health equity staff
   - Budget allocation

2. Stratified Reporting
   - Quality measures by demographics
   - Regular disparity analysis
   - Public transparency

3. Reducing Disparities
   - Targeted interventions
   - Root cause analysis
   - Community partnerships

4. Cultural Responsiveness
   - Interpreter services
   - Translated materials
   - Culturally tailored programs

5. Workforce Development
   - Diversity hiring goals
   - Cultural competency training
   - Community health worker programs

Benefits of HEA:
- Market differentiation
- CMS bonus eligibility
- Regulatory advantage
- Member trust

==================================================
BEST PRACTICES FOR MA PLANS
==================================================

1. Stratify Everything
   - Run all quality reports by race/ethnicity
   - Identify which groups have largest gaps
   - Prioritize high-disparity measures

2. Target Interventions
   - Don't use one-size-fits-all approaches
   - Tailor outreach by cultural preferences
   - Partner with community organizations

3. Remove Barriers
   - Provide transportation
   - Waive cost-sharing for preventive services
   - Offer evening/weekend appointments

4. Engage Community
   - Partner with faith-based organizations
   - Hire community health workers from target populations
   - Host events in underserved neighborhoods

5. Measure and Improve
   - Track disparity metrics monthly
   - Set explicit disparity reduction goals
   - Report progress to board

==================================================
COMMON PITFALLS TO AVOID
==================================================

❌ Incomplete Data Collection
- If you don't collect race/ethnicity, you can't measure disparities
- Aim for >95% data completeness

❌ Analysis Paralysis
- Don't wait for perfect data to act
- Start with available data and improve over time

❌ Ignoring Root Causes
- Disparities aren't about individual behavior
- Address systemic barriers (transportation, cost, access)

❌ Lack of Accountability
- Set specific, measurable disparity reduction goals
- Tie executive compensation to equity metrics

❌ Cultural Incompetence
- Using English-only materials for Spanish-speaking members
- Not offering interpreter services
- Ignoring cultural beliefs about healthcare
