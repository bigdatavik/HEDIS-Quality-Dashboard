DATA SOURCE DESCRIPTIONS - HEDIS Quality Measures

=== GENIE SPACE ===
The Genie Space provides natural language access to structured HEDIS quality data tables in the humana_quality.hedis_gold schema.

Tables available:
- quality_summary: Overall quality performance metrics and star rating projections
- measure_performance: Performance by individual HEDIS measure with compliance rates
- member_measures: Member-level measure compliance and gap status
- gap_closure_tracking: Gap in care status, closure dates, and trending

The Genie Space should be used for:
- Aggregate statistics and trends
- Cross-measure analysis and comparisons
- Time-series queries (monthly, quarterly trends)
- Cohort identification and segmentation
- Performance benchmarking

Example use cases:
- "What percentage of members closed BCS gaps in Q4 2024?"
- "Compare CDC performance across different age groups"
- "Show me top 10 measures by gap count"
- "What's our trend in overall compliance over the last 6 months?"
- "How many members became compliant this month vs last month?"
- "Show me measures where compliance dropped"

Genie Space advantages:
✅ Natural language queries (no SQL knowledge needed)
✅ Fast aggregate calculations
✅ Cross-table joins handled automatically
✅ Time-based filtering and trending
✅ Great for exploratory analysis

=== UC FUNCTIONS ===
Unity Catalog Functions provide fast, indexed lookups for specific members, measures, and gaps.

All functions are in catalog: humana_quality, schema: hedis_gold

---

lookup_member(member_id STRING)
  Purpose: Get complete member demographics and current quality status
  Returns: Single member record with:
    - member_id: Unique identifier
    - first_name, last_name: Member name
    - age: Current age
    - sex: M/F
    - eligible_measures_count: Number of applicable HEDIS measures
    - compliant_measures_count: Number of measures where member is compliant
    - open_gaps_count: Number of measures with open gaps
    - overall_status: 'Compliant', 'Partial', or 'Non-Compliant'
  Use when: Need basic member information and summary quality status
  Example: lookup_member('M000001')
  
---

lookup_member_measures(member_id STRING)
  Purpose: Get detailed quality measure compliance for a specific member
  Returns: All applicable HEDIS measures for the member with:
    - member_id: Unique identifier
    - measure_code: HEDIS measure code (BCS, CDC, CBP, etc.)
    - measure_name: Full measure name
    - is_compliant: TRUE/FALSE
    - compliance_status: 'Compliant', 'Gap', or 'At Risk'
    - last_service_date: Date of most recent qualifying service
    - gap_closure_action: Recommended action if gap exists
    - due_date: Date by which service should be completed
  Use when: Need complete quality profile for a member
  Example: lookup_member_measures('M000001')
  Typical result: 3-8 measures per member (depending on eligibility)

---

lookup_member_gaps(member_id STRING)
  Purpose: Get all open gaps in care for a specific member
  Returns: Only measures where member has open gaps:
    - member_id: Unique identifier
    - measure_code: HEDIS measure code
    - measure_name: Full measure name
    - gap_type: 'Missing Service', 'Overdue', or 'At Risk'
    - gap_closure_action: Specific recommended action
    - days_until_due: Days remaining until end of measurement period
    - priority: 'High', 'Medium', or 'Low'
  Use when: Care management needs gap closure list for outreach
  Example: lookup_member_gaps('M000001')
  Typical result: 0-4 gaps per member
  Note: Returns empty if member is fully compliant

---

members_with_gap(measure_code STRING)
  Purpose: Find all members with an open gap for a specific measure
  Returns: List of all members with gap:
    - member_id: Unique identifier
    - first_name, last_name: Member name
    - age, sex: Demographics
    - gap_type: Type of gap
    - gap_closure_action: Recommended action
    - days_until_due: Days remaining
    - contact_phone: Member phone for outreach
  Use when: Need to identify outreach cohort for gap closure campaign
  Example: members_with_gap('BCS')
  Typical result: Varies by measure (could be 100-5000 members)
  Best practices:
    - Use for targeted outreach campaigns
    - Prioritize by days_until_due
    - Filter by high-priority measures first (CDC, CBP, COL)

---

lookup_measure_performance(measure_code STRING)
  Purpose: Get overall performance statistics for a specific measure
  Returns: Aggregate performance metrics:
    - measure_code: HEDIS measure code
    - measure_name: Full measure name
    - eligible_members: Total members eligible for measure
    - compliant_members: Count of compliant members
    - gap_members: Count with open gaps
    - compliance_rate_pct: Percentage compliant (0-100)
    - star_rating: Projected star rating (1-5)
    - trend: 'Improving', 'Declining', or 'Stable'
    - revenue_impact: Dollar impact of improving to next star level
  Use when: Need measure-level summary for reporting
  Example: lookup_measure_performance('CDC')
  Use cases:
    - Executive dashboards
    - Monthly reporting
    - Identifying underperforming measures

---

members_at_risk(measure_code STRING)
  Purpose: Find members at risk of missing measure requirements (proactive identification)
  Returns: Members who are currently compliant but at risk:
    - member_id: Unique identifier
    - first_name, last_name: Member name
    - last_service_date: Date of most recent qualifying service
    - days_since_service: Days since last service
    - risk_level: 'High', 'Medium', or 'Low'
    - proactive_action: Recommended preventive outreach
    - projected_gap_date: Date when gap will open if no action
  Use when: Proactive outreach planning to prevent gaps
  Example: members_at_risk('CBP')
  Typical result: 50-500 members depending on measure
  Best practices:
    - Run monthly for early intervention
    - Focus on high-value measures
    - Contact members before gap opens

=== UC FUNCTIONS - PERFORMANCE CHARACTERISTICS ===

Speed:
- Individual lookups (lookup_member, lookup_member_measures): < 1 second
- Filtered searches (members_with_gap, members_at_risk): 1-5 seconds
- Aggregate queries (lookup_measure_performance): < 1 second

Data Freshness:
- Updated nightly at 2 AM
- Claims typically process within 7-14 days of service
- Real-time gap closure may have 1-day lag

Function Permissions:
- All functions have EXECUTE permission granted to 'account users'
- App service principals have access automatically
- No additional permissions needed for end users

=== COMBINING DATA SOURCES ===

For complex questions, use multiple sources:

Example 1: "Show me member M000001's BCS gap and explain how to close it"
  Step 1: lookup_member_gaps('M000001') → Get specific gap details
  Step 2: Knowledge Assistant search "BCS gap closure procedure" → Get process
  Result: Data-driven answer with actionable guidance

Example 2: "Which measures are underperforming and what can we do?"
  Step 1: Genie Space → "Show me measures with compliance < 75%"
  Step 2: Knowledge Assistant → "Best practices for improving HEDIS compliance"
  Result: Identifies problem areas with improvement strategies

Example 3: "Find high-risk diabetic members and explain outreach strategy"
  Step 1: members_at_risk('CDC') → Get list of at-risk members
  Step 2: Knowledge Assistant → "CDC member outreach best practices"
  Result: Targeted cohort with proven outreach approach

=== DATA QUALITY NOTES ===

Known Limitations:
- Claims processing lag: 7-14 days typical
- Member eligibility updates: Monthly
- Measure specifications: Annual updates (effective January 1)

Data Validation:
- All member_ids validated against enrollment table
- Measure codes restricted to valid HEDIS codes
- Dates validated for measurement year
- Duplicate gap records removed

Troubleshooting:
- If gap doesn't show as closed after service: Check claim processing status
- If member not found: Verify enrollment status and member_id format
- If unexpected measure eligibility: Review member demographics against measure criteria
