## What is a Gap Closure Initiative?

### Definition

A **gap closure initiative** is a targeted program where a health plan allocates budget and resources to close quality measure gaps (members not meeting HEDIS compliance criteria) to:
- Improve Star Ratings
- Reduce medical costs
- Improve member health outcomes
- Meet value-based contract requirements

### Business Context

**The Scenario:**
A Medicare Advantage or Commercial health plan discovers that they have quality gaps that are:
1. **Hurting Star Ratings** → Losing $9M+ in bonus payments
2. **Creating medical cost risk** → Uncontrolled chronic conditions lead to expensive ER visits/hospitalizations
3. **Risking contract compliance** → Employer groups require minimum quality thresholds
4. **Damaging reputation** → Poor public ratings affect enrollment

**Leadership Response:**
> *"We need to close these gaps. How much will it cost, and what will we get back?"*

This triggers the allocation of an **initiative budget** (typically $100K - $10M depending on plan size and gap volume).

## Budget Allocation

### Typical Budget: $200K Initiative for 10,000 Member Plan

**Budget Breakdown:**

| Category | Amount | % of Budget | Details |
|----------|--------|-------------|---------|
| **Outreach Staff** | $80,000 | 40% | 2-3 RNs or care coordinators for 6 months |
| **Member Incentives** | $50,000 | 25% | Gift cards, transportation, co-pay assistance |
| **Mobile Health Services** | $40,000 | 20% | Mobile mammography, pop-up clinics |
| **Provider Incentives** | $20,000 | 10% | Bonus payments for closing gaps |
| **Technology/Analytics** | $10,000 | 5% | Lists, tracking tools, predictive models |
| **TOTAL** | **$200,000** | 100% | |

### Budget Scaling by Plan Size

| Plan Size | Typical Budget | Per-Member Cost | Expected Gap Closure |
|-----------|----------------|-----------------|---------------------|
| 10,000 members | $200K | $20/member | 500-750 gaps |
| 50,000 members | $750K | $15/member | 3,000-4,500 gaps |
| 100,000 members | $1.2M | $12/member | 6,000-9,000 gaps |
| 500,000 members | $4.5M | $9/member | 30,000-45,000 gaps |

*Note: Economies of scale reduce per-member cost*

## Detailed Budget Categories

### 1. Outreach Staff ($80K - 40% of budget)

**What It Buys:**
- 2-3 registered nurses (RN) or licensed practical nurses (LPN) for 6 months
- Typical rate: $50-70/hour fully loaded
- Work: Telephonic outreach, gap identification, appointment scheduling

**Activities:**
- Call members with open gaps
- Explain why screening is important
- Schedule appointments with PCPs
- Arrange transportation if needed
- Follow up after appointments
- Document all interactions

**Productivity Targets:**
- 20-30 member contacts per day per nurse
- 60-70% contact rate (member answers)
- 40-50% agreement to schedule appointment
- 30-35% actual completion rate

**Math:**
```
2 nurses × 6 months × 20 workdays/month × 25 calls/day = 6,000 outreach attempts
Contact rate: 60% = 3,600 conversations
Schedule rate: 45% = 1,620 appointments scheduled
Completion rate: 35% = 567 gaps closed

Cost per gap closed: $80,000 / 567 = $141
```

### 2. Member Incentives ($50K - 25% of budget)

**Gift Cards ($25,000):**
- $25 per completed screening
- Walmart, Target, Amazon gift cards
- Drives 15-20% increase in completion rates
- 1,000 cards = $25,000
- Used for: Mammograms, colonoscopies, diabetic eye exams, lab tests

**Transportation Vouchers ($15,000):**
- Uber/Lyft credits
- Average ride: $30 round-trip
- 500 rides = $15,000
- Critical for members without vehicles
- Especially important in rural areas

**Co-Pay Waivers ($10,000):**
- Waive $10-50 co-pay for preventive services
- Removes financial barrier
- 200 waivers @ $50 = $10,000
- Most impactful for low-income members

**Example Member Journey:**
```
Member: Maria, 62, diabetic, hasn't had HbA1c test in 18 months

Barriers:
- No car (rural area)
- $35 co-pay for lab work
- Doesn't understand why test is important

Solution:
- Nurse calls, explains diabetes complications risk
- Offers $25 gift card for completing test
- Provides Uber voucher ($30)
- Waives co-pay ($35)

Total cost: $90
Gap closed: ✅
Avoided future ER visit: $8,000 saved
```

### 3. Mobile Health Services ($40K - 20% of budget)

**Mobile Mammography Van ($25,000):**
- Rent van for 2 weeks
- Set up at senior centers, churches, workplaces
- Typical rate: $2,000/day + $500/day for technician
- Can screen 50 women/day
- 10 days × 50 = 500 screenings
- Cost per screening: $50 (vs $250 at hospital)

**Pop-Up Flu Shot Clinics ($10,000):**
- Partner with pharmacies
- Set up at community events
- Cost: $25/shot × 400 members = $10,000
- Convenience drives compliance

**In-Home Assessments ($5,000):**
- Send nurse to homebound members
- Complete multiple measures in one visit
- Cost: $150/visit × 33 members = $5,000
- High-value for frail/elderly

### 4. Provider Incentives ($20K - 10% of budget)

**Gap Closure Bonuses:**
- Pay PCP $100-300 per gap closed
- Motivates provider engagement
- Typically: $200/gap × 100 gaps = $20,000

**Structures:**
```
Option A: Flat fee
- $200 per gap closed
- Simple, easy to track
- Popular with small practices

Option B: Tiered incentive
- Close 1-10 gaps: $150/gap
- Close 11-25 gaps: $200/gap
- Close 26+ gaps: $250/gap
- Rewards high performers

Option C: All-or-nothing
- Hit 85% compliance: $10,000 bonus
- Hit 90% compliance: $20,000 bonus
- Drives focus on overall performance
```

### 5. Technology/Analytics ($10K - 5% of budget)

**Gap Lists & Tracking:**
- Export member lists with open gaps
- Predictive scoring (likelihood to close)
- Outreach campaign management software
- Cost: $3,000

**Supplemental Data:**
- Claims data feeds
- Lab result data
- Pharmacy fill history
- Cost: $5,000

**Reporting/Dashboards:**
- Weekly progress tracking
- ROI calculations
- Executive reporting
- Cost: $2,000

## Outreach Tactics

### Telephonic Outreach (Most Common)

**Process:**
1. **Predictive dialing** - Call members with open gaps
2. **Nurse conversation** - Explain gap, assess barriers
3. **Schedule appointment** - Coordinate with PCP office
4. **Send reminder** - Text/email 24 hours before
5. **Follow-up call** - Confirm completion

**Best Practices:**
- Call 3-5 times before marking "unable to reach"
- Call evenings/weekends for working members
- Use preferred language (Spanish, Mandarin, etc.)
- Warm transfer to PCP office
- Document barriers (transportation, cost, fear)

**Script Example:**
```
"Hi Mrs. Johnson, this is Sarah, a nurse from Blue Cross. I'm calling because 
our records show you're due for your annual mammogram. This is an important 
screening that can catch breast cancer early. Have you had a mammogram in 
the past 2 years?

[Listen to response]

Great! I'd like to help you schedule that. We can even provide a free ride 
to your appointment and a $25 gift card after you complete the screening. 
Would you like me to schedule that for you today?"
```

### Mailed Outreach

**Personalized Letters:**
- List of specific gaps member has
- Explanation of why each matters
- Pre-scheduled appointment (member can call to confirm/change)
- $25 gift card mention
- QR code to schedule online

**Effectiveness:**
- 5-10% response rate
- Works well for educated/engaged members
- Cost: $2-5 per letter

**Sample Letter:**
```
Dear Mr. Smith,

As a member with diabetes, it's important that you have an HbA1c blood 
test at least once per year. Our records show your last test was 
15 months ago.

Why this matters: This simple blood test shows how well your diabetes 
is controlled. Uncontrolled diabetes can lead to serious complications 
like heart disease, kidney damage, and vision loss.

What we'll do for you:
✓ Schedule appointment at your doctor's office
✓ Send you an Uber to get there
✓ Mail you a $25 Walmart gift card after your test

Call us at 1-800-XXX-XXXX to schedule, or scan the QR code below.

Sincerely,
Your Blue Cross Care Team
```

### Text Message Campaigns

**SMS Outreach:**
- Short, actionable messages
- Include link to schedule
- Follow-up reminders
- 25-35% open rate
- 10-15% click-through rate

**Example Texts:**
```
Text 1 (Initial):
"Hi Maria, you're due for your annual mammogram. We'll give you a 
$25 gift card + free ride. Schedule here: [link] -Blue Cross"

Text 2 (Reminder, 3 days later):
"Reminder: Schedule your mammogram to get your $25 gift card. 
Only takes 20 minutes! [link]"

Text 3 (Appointment reminder):
"Your mammogram is tomorrow at 10am at ABC Imaging. Your Uber 
will arrive at 9:30am. See you there!"

Text 4 (Post-appointment):
"Thanks for completing your mammogram! Your $25 gift card is 
in the mail. Questions? Call 1-800-XXX-XXXX"
```

### Mobile/Pop-Up Events

**Strategy:** Bring care to members (remove access barrier)

**Event Types:**
1. **Mobile Mammography** - Mall parking lots, community centers
2. **Health Fairs** - Flu shots, BP checks, diabetic screenings
3. **Provider Site Takeovers** - Plan staff at PCP office
4. **Faith-Based Partnerships** - Screenings at churches

**Example Event:**
```
Event: Mobile Mammography at Walmart
- Saturday, 9am-5pm
- Park mobile van in lot
- 8 appointment slots/hour × 8 hours = 64 mammograms
- Walk-ins accepted
- $25 gift card on-site

Promotion:
- Postcards to 500 women with BCS gaps
- Social media posts
- Flyers in store
- Local radio ad

Results:
- 48 scheduled appointments (75% show rate = 36 completed)
- 12 walk-ins completed
- Total: 48 mammograms
- Cost: $2,500 (van) + $1,200 (gift cards) = $3,700
- Cost per gap closed: $77
```

## ROI Calculations

### ROI Formula

```
ROI = (Total Value Generated - Total Investment) / Total Investment × 100%

Where Total Value = Avoided Costs + Revenue Gains + Quality Bonuses
```

### Example 1: Small Initiative

**Investment:**
- Budget: $50,000
- Target: Close 250 gaps in diabetes care (CDC measure)

**Returns:**

**1. Avoided Medical Costs:**
```
Historical data: 8% of members with uncontrolled diabetes have 
ER visit or hospitalization within 12 months

Without intervention:
- 250 members × 8% = 20 ER visits/hospitalizations
- Average cost: $8,000 per event
- Total cost: 20 × $8,000 = $160,000

With intervention (gaps closed, diabetes controlled):
- Estimated reduction: 50% fewer events
- Events avoided: 10
- Savings: 10 × $8,000 = $80,000
```

**2. Star Rating Impact:**
```
Plan has 10,000 members
Current diabetes care rate: 86.5% (3.5 stars)
Need 88% for 4 stars (250 gap improvement gets to 88.5%)

Bonus payment:
- Revenue: $180M/year
- Bonus rate: 5%
- Bonus amount: $9M/year

Attribution (this initiative): $9M × 2% improvement = $180K
(Conservative: assume initiative contributes 2% of overall improvement)
```

**3. Total ROI:**
```
Investment: $50,000

Returns:
- Avoided costs: $80,000
- Star rating bonus (attributed): $180,000
- Total: $260,000

ROI: ($260,000 - $50,000) / $50,000 = 420%

Payback period: 2.3 months
```

### Example 2: Large Multi-Measure Initiative

**Investment:**
- Budget: $1.2M
- Target: 6,000 gaps across 5 measures
- Plan size: 100,000 members

**Returns:**

**1. Avoided Costs:**
```
Diabetes (2,000 gaps): 
- 160 ER visits prevented × $8,000 = $1.28M

Breast Cancer (1,500 gaps):
- 3 cancers caught early vs late stage
- Savings per early detection: $100,000
- Total: 3 × $100,000 = $300K

Blood Pressure (1,500 gaps):
- 45 strokes/heart attacks prevented
- Savings: 45 × $50,000 = $2.25M

Colorectal Screening (1,000 gaps):
- 2 cancers caught early
- Savings: 2 × $150,000 = $300K

Total Avoided Costs: $4.13M
```

**2. Star Rating Bonus:**
```
Improvement from 3.5 → 4.0 stars

Revenue: $1.8B/year (100K members × $1,500/month × 12)
Bonus: 5% × $1.8B = $90M/year

Full ROI (if solely responsible): 7,400%
Conservative attribution (25%): $22.5M
```

**3. Total ROI:**
```
Investment: $1.2M

Returns (Year 1):
- Avoided costs: $4.13M
- Star rating bonus (25% attribution): $22.5M
- Total: $26.63M

ROI: ($26.63M - $1.2M) / $1.2M = 2,119%

Payback period: 16 days
```

## Success Metrics

### Primary KPIs

| Metric | Definition | Target | Industry Benchmark |
|--------|------------|--------|-------------------|
| **Contact Rate** | % of members successfully reached | 60-70% | 50-65% |
| **Engagement Rate** | % who agree to intervention | 45-55% | 40-50% |
| **Completion Rate** | % who complete screening | 35-45% | 30-40% |
| **Overall Gap Closure** | % of targeted gaps closed | 30-40% | 25-35% |
| **Cost per Gap Closed** | Total spend / gaps closed | <$200 | $150-$300 |
| **ROI** | Value generated / investment | >300% | 200-500% |

## Real Program Examples

### Program 1: "Healthy Start" - Diabetes Focus

**Plan:** Regional MA plan, 25,000 members  
**Timeline:** 6 months (July - December)  
**Budget:** $300K

**Target:**
- 1,200 diabetic members with gaps in HbA1c testing, eye exams, or kidney tests

**Tactics:**
1. **Hired 4 nurses** for telephonic outreach ($160K)
2. **$25 gift cards** for completed tests ($60K)
3. **Free A1C testing** at retail clinics ($50K)
4. **Provider bonuses** ($30K)

**Results:**
- 897 gaps closed (75% success rate)
- CDC measure: 84% → 90%
- Star rating: 3.5 → 4.0
- Quality bonus gained: $11M/year
- ROI: 3,567%

**Key Success Factors:**
- Targeted single condition (focused effort)
- Retail clinic partnerships (convenient access)
- Generous gift card ($25 drives action)
- Consistent follow-up (average 3.2 calls per member)

### Program 2: "Mammogram Marathon" - Cancer Screening

**Plan:** Large MA plan, 150,000 members  
**Timeline:** 3 months (August - October)  
**Budget:** $500K

**Target:**
- 5,000 women ages 50-74 overdue for mammogram

**Tactics:**
1. **Mobile mammography vans** - 4 vans × 12 weeks ($240K)
2. **Event locations** - Walmarts, churches, community centers
3. **Heavy promotion** - Postcards, texts, local radio ($100K)
4. **$50 gift cards** ($150K)
5. **On-site scheduling team** ($10K)

**Results:**
- 2,847 mammograms completed (57% success rate)
- BCS measure: 88% → 93%
- 8 early-stage cancers detected
- Estimated 8 lives saved
- Star rating: Maintained 4.5 (prevented decline)
- ROI: 980% (avoided treatment costs + maintained bonus)

**Key Success Factors:**
- Removed ALL barriers (came to members, free, convenient)
- Large gift card incentive ($50)
- Community partnerships
- Weekend/evening events

### Program 3: "High-Risk Navigator" - Multi-Measure

**Plan:** Small MA plan, 8,000 members  
**Timeline:** 12 months (ongoing program)  
**Budget:** $180K/year

**Target:**
- 500 members with 3+ open gaps AND high medical costs

**Tactics:**
1. **2 dedicated RN navigators** ($140K)
2. **In-home visits** for complex members
3. **Concierge scheduling** - navigator does everything
4. **Transportation coordination** ($25K)
5. **Barrier removal** - co-pay assistance, language services ($15K)

**Results:**
- 347 members closed all gaps (69%)
- Average 3.8 gaps closed per member
- Total: 1,319 gaps closed
- Multiple measures improved 2-4 points each
- ER utilization -23% for these members
- Star rating: 3.0 → 3.5 (avoided contract termination)
- ROI: 1,150%

**Key Success Factors:**
- High-touch personal approach
- Addressed social barriers (not just clinical)
- Trusted relationship (same nurse throughout)
- In-home visits (met members where they are)

## Common Pitfalls

### 1. Starting Too Late
- **Mistake:** Launch initiative in November (measurement year ends December 31)
- **Impact:** Not enough time to complete screenings
- **Fix:** Start in July or earlier

### 2. Targeting Wrong Gaps
- **Mistake:** Focus on easy gaps, not impactful ones
- **Impact:** Close 500 gaps, Star Rating doesn't move
- **Fix:** Model which measures are close to cut points

### 3. Ignoring Member Barriers
- **Mistake:** "Just call them and tell them to go"
- **Impact:** Low completion rates (10-15%)
- **Fix:** Remove barriers (transportation, cost, convenience)

### 4. No Provider Engagement
- **Mistake:** Assume PCPs will prioritize gaps
- **Impact:** Appointments scheduled but members not counseled
- **Fix:** Provider bonuses, report cards, direct communication

### 5. Poor Follow-Up
- **Mistake:** Call once, give up if no answer
- **Impact:** 50% of closeable gaps never reached
- **Fix:** 3-5 call attempts, multiple channels (phone, text, mail)

### 6. Weak Incentives
- **Mistake:** "$5 gift card should be enough"
- **Impact:** Incentive too small to drive behavior change
- **Fix:** $20-50 range drives action (proven in behavioral economics)

### 7. No ROI Tracking
- **Mistake:** "We closed gaps, that's success"
- **Impact:** Can't justify budget for next year
- **Fix:** Track costs, savings, and Star Rating impact explicitly

## Next Steps

### Planning Your Initiative

**Phase 1: Assessment (Weeks 1-2)**
1. Identify total gaps by measure
2. Segment by closability (easy/medium/hard)
3. Calculate potential Star Rating impact
4. Project ROI

**Phase 2: Design (Weeks 3-4)**
1. Set budget
2. Choose tactics (outreach, mobile, incentives)
3. Hire/train staff
4. Set up tracking

**Phase 3: Launch (Weeks 5-6)**
1. Begin outreach
2. Monitor metrics weekly
3. Adjust tactics based on results

**Phase 4: Execute (Weeks 7-20)**
1. Scale what works
2. Stop what doesn't
3. Weekly reporting to leadership

**Phase 5: Close & Report (Weeks 21-24)**
1. Final push in December
2. Calculate final ROI
3. Plan next year's initiative
