## 🎯 Publishing Strategy

### **1. LinkedIn Article (Primary)**
1. Open `LINKEDIN_ARTICLE_READY.txt`
2. Copy all content
3. Go to LinkedIn → "Write article"
4. Paste content
5. Add cover image (dashboard screenshot or Unsplash)
6. Publish
7. Share to feed using template from `LINKEDIN_POST_TEMPLATE.md`

**Best Time:** Tuesday-Thursday, 7-9 AM PST or 11 AM-1 PM PST

### **2. Medium (Secondary - After 1 Week)**
1. Use `BUSINESS_VALUE_BLOG.md` as source
2. Convert markdown to Medium format
3. Cross-post for SEO and broader reach
4. Add canonical link back to LinkedIn article

### **3. Databricks Blog (Optional)**
- Submit `BUSINESS_VALUE_BLOG.md` to Databricks marketing team
- Position as customer success story / reference architecture
- Great for internal enablement and external thought leadership
