## 📌 Best Practices for LinkedIn as Databricks Employee

### ✅ DO:
- Share real customer outcomes and metrics
- Focus on thought leadership and education
- Emphasize open-source and knowledge sharing
- Invite discussion and feedback
- Use "we" when referring to Databricks work
- Tag relevant colleagues if appropriate
- Use 3-5 relevant hashtags
- Include a call-to-action (read, comment, connect)

### ❌ DON'T:
- Make it sound like a sales pitch
- Compare directly to competitors by name
- Use aggressive sales language ("buy now", "contact sales")
- Overpromise or hype
- Make it all about Databricks—focus on customer value
- Forget to add your perspective/learning
- Post without engaging with comments

## 🎨 Formatting Tips

**Use Line Breaks:**
- Short paragraphs (2-3 lines max)
- Blank lines between sections
- Makes it scannable on mobile

**Use Emojis Sparingly:**
- ✅ One at the start of each metric
- 📊 One for the title/hook
- 👇 One for the call-to-action
- Don't overdo it

**Lead with Value:**
- First 2-3 lines show up in feed
- Make them compelling
- Get to the point fast

**End with Questions:**
- Drives engagement
- Shows you want discussion, not just attention
